# Funder logo sources

These marks identify grant funders and programs; they do not imply endorsement.

- `imls.png`: Institute of Museum and Library Services logo, U.S. federal government work, sourced from Wikimedia Commons.
- `nrf-korea.jpg`: National Research Foundation of Korea English logo, sourced from Wikimedia Commons.
- `ua.svg`: existing University of Alabama Capstone A asset from this site.
- `ache.png`: Alabama Commission on Higher Education logo from ache.edu.
- `sec.png`: Southeastern Conference logo, sourced from Wikimedia Commons.
- `aera.png`: existing American Educational Research Association asset from this site.
- `aims.png`: AIMS Collaboratory logo from aimscollaboratory.org.
- `nsf.png`: National Science Foundation logo, U.S. federal government work, sourced from Wikimedia Commons.
- `nih.png`: National Institutes of Health logo, U.S. federal government mark, sourced from Wikimedia Commons.
- `ies.png`: Institute of Education Sciences logo, U.S. federal government work, sourced from Wikimedia Commons; cropped to the IES sunburst mark (added 2026-11-13).
- `naed.jpg`: National Academy of Education logo from naeducation.org (added 2026-11-13).
- `spencer.png`: Spencer Foundation wordmark from spencer.org, recolored from white to dark for a light tile (added 2026-11-13).
- `russell-sage.svg`: Russell Sage Foundation mark from russellsage.org, recolored from white to navy for a light tile (added 2026-11-13).
- `charles-koch.svg`: Charles Koch Foundation circular signature mark, redrawn at vector resolution from the official site favicon design and the signature path on charleskochfoundation.org (added 2026-11-13).
- `sony.svg`: Sony wordmark, public domain, sourced from Wikimedia Commons (added 2026-11-13).
- `microsoft.svg`: Microsoft logo (2012), public domain, sourced from Wikimedia Commons; used for Microsoft Research (added 2026-11-13).
- `asmodee.png`: Asmodee logo, public domain, sourced from Wikimedia Commons and cropped (added 2026-11-13).
- `raais.png`: The RAAIS Foundation logo from raais.org, recolored to dark for a light tile (added 2026-11-13).
- `sentient.svg`: Sentient Foundation mark from sentient.foundation, dark fill for a light tile (added 2026-11-13).
- `aims.png` is also used for the Digital Promise AIMS EduData program.

All marks remain the property or trademarks of their respective organizations and are used only for factual identification.
